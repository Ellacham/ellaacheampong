About Me

I am a PhD student in Social Policy at the Heller School for Social Policy and Management, Brandeis University, concentrating in Economic and Racial Equity. My work connects educational inequality and economic opportunity with artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and applied data science.

My current social policy research examines educational pathway disruptions in Ghana. Alongside this work, I continue to pursue machine learning, fraud detection, digital identity protection, and cybersecurity analytics.

I am also a Research Assistant at the Center for Global Development and Sustainability at Brandeis, in its Cyber Innovation & Livelihood Security division. Before my doctoral studies, I worked in data science and analytics at Consolidated Bank Ghana (2021–2023) and as a cybersecurity analyst at Miracle Cyber (2024).
