Featured Project · Research Prototype

FraudLens™

Experimental machine-learning framework for detecting potentially fraudulent financial transactions, comparing Random Forest and XGBoost, and presenting risk scores through a Streamlit research demo.

Current status: research prototype, not a deployed banking system.

Dataset
IEEE-CIS Fraud Detection dataset
Models
Random Forest and XGBoost
Tools
Python · XGBoost · Random Forest · scikit-learn · Streamlit

Overview

FraudLens explores how machine learning can help flag potentially fraudulent financial transactions. It is an experimental framework built to study fraud classification, not a production fraud-prevention system.

Data

The models are trained and evaluated on the IEEE-CIS Fraud Detection dataset, a public benchmark of online payment transactions labelled as fraudulent or legitimate.

Modeling approach

The project involved feature engineering on the transaction data, followed by training and comparing Random Forest and XGBoost classifiers and evaluating their performance.

Research demo

A Streamlit research demo presents the models’ risk scores for transactions.
