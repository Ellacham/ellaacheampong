Manuscript in Preparation · AI, Cybersecurity & Digital Trust

Bank-Related Identity Theft in the U.S.

Examining bank-related identity theft and associated financial losses.

Status
Manuscript in preparation
Presented
Brandeis Global Cybersecurity & AI Conference, Brandeis University, April 2025

Overview

An empirical study examining how different types of bank-related identity theft influence the financial losses experienced by victims. The work uses statistical analysis of identity theft data to compare how different fraud types relate to the size of those losses.

Presentation

The work was presented at the Brandeis Global Cybersecurity & AI Conference in a talk on identity theft trends and data-driven approaches for detecting fraud in digital banking systems.

Manuscript

The abstract, data and methods will be shared here once the manuscript is ready for release.
